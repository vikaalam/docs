Everything an operator opens. Owned by `task` and `process`, except the first one.

## Attention

<img src="/images/screens/attention.webp" alt="Attention — the union of six modules' queues" noZoom />

Cross-module by design. It unions the queue promises of six modules and **owns none of them** — which
is why it is specified with the patterns rather than with a module, and why it must never grow logic
of its own.

## My tasks

<img src="/images/screens/my-tasks.webp" alt="My tasks — one operator's work, grouped by domain" noZoom />

`task` — *"a unit of human work: assignment, lifecycle, SLA, form, comments, attachments."* Grouped
by business domain.

## My tasks — compliance view

<img src="/images/screens/my-tasks-compliance.webp" alt="My tasks, filtered to the compliance domain" noZoom />

The same screen scoped to one team's work. Queue scope is the third dimension of authorization —
enforced on the server, not by filtering in the browser.

## My tasks — board

<img src="/images/screens/my-tasks-kanban.webp" alt="The same tasks grouped by workflow state" noZoom />

Same data as the list, grouped by workflow state instead of listed. **A view option, not a new
screen.**

## Queues

<img src="/images/screens/queues.webp" alt="Queues — depth, unassigned work, SLA health" noZoom />

`task` — team queues: depth, unassigned work, SLA health. The scope is `queue:<domain>`.

## Task detail

<img src="/images/screens/task-detail.webp" alt="Task detail — outcome form, evidence, provided links, activity" noZoom />

One work unit opened: the outcome form, evidence, **provided links with their provenance shown**, and
the activity history.

<Note>
  A link that arrived in an envelope is the one URL a reviewer is trained to click. The interface
  always states where it came from rather than appearing to vouch for it.
</Note>

## Cases

<img src="/images/screens/cases.webp" alt="Cases — running instances, their step, and what each waits on" noZoom />

`process` — *"versioned definitions and stateful instances; steps automatic, human, waiting, branching,
or parallel."* Answers *"where is this case, and why is it stuck?"*

## Case detail

<img src="/images/screens/case-detail.webp" alt="Case detail — ordered steps, pinned version, armed timers, sub-tasks" noZoom />

A stateful, resumable instance: ordered steps, a **pinned definition version**, armed timers, and the
sub-tasks handed to people.

The one drawn here is waiting on a source-of-funds document past its deadline — which is why it
escalated.

## The compliance dashboard

<img src="/images/screens/dashboard.webp" alt="A per-team landing screen" noZoom />

**Not a module.** A per-team landing that aggregates open reviews, SLA breaches and regulatory
filings. Every card is owned by a real module; **this screen owns none of it.**

## A second set exists, scoped to Operations

Eight further screens were designed for the Operations persona against the **measured event
catalogue** rather than against the module documents — a distinct sidebar, and a distinct rule.

<Note>
  **Four of them are named after business entities and none of them lists one.** Deposits, parties,
  virtual accounts and instructions each show the *work* and the *signals* about their subject, and
  each says so on the screen — because the company-agnostic rule erodes exactly there.
</Note>

They are rendered where their reader is, rather than duplicated here.
