This tab is for the person **wiring something up**. It carries the mechanisms in enough detail to
implement them, and it stops there.

<Note>
  **Reference says *how*. Engineering says *why*.**

  Where a mechanism exists because of a decision — and most of these do — the reference page states
  the mechanism and links to the decision record rather than restating the argument.
</Note>

## The one sentence worth carrying out of this tab

<Warning>
  **The module guard is derived from the route. The resource permission is declared per operation.**

  That asymmetry is the design: an annotation you can forget is a check that **fails open**, so the
  guard most likely to be forgotten is taken out of human hands entirely.
</Warning>
